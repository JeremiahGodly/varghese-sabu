Curated Legal Doctrines & Constitutional Milestones

Deep-dive statutory facts and historical precedents that define practice before the High Court of Kerala and the Supreme Court of India.

Admiralty & Shipping FACT 01

Maritime Lien Adheres to the Vessel Itself

Under the Admiralty Act 2017, a maritime lien for crew wages, salvage, or bunker supplies travels with the ship, even if ownership or flag is changed in a foreign registry. Filing an action *in rem* freezes the hull upon calling at Cochin Port.

Constitutional Law FACT 02

Basic Structure Was Born in Kasaragod

*Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973)* originated regarding land reforms in Edneer Mutt, Kasaragod. The landmark 13-Judge Supreme Court decision established that the core identity and basic structure of our Constitution cannot be repealed.

Liberty & Bail FACT 03

Bail is the Rule, Jail is the Exception

Coined by Kerala's own legendary Supreme Court Justice V.R. Krishna Iyer in *State of Rajasthan v. Balchand (1977)*. Incarceration before conviction damages livelihood, dignity, and family, and cannot be inflicted without compelling flight risk.

Arbitration Act 1996 FACT 04

Section 9 Pre-Arbitration Injunctions

A party does not need to wait for an arbitrator to be appointed before seeking an asset freeze or restraining order. The Commercial Court can grant urgent interim protection under Section 9, provided arbitration is initiated within 90 days.

Evidence & Cyber FACT 05

Electronic Evidence u/s 63 BSA

Under Section 63 BSA 2023 (formerly Section 65B Evidence Act), electronic records like WhatsApp chats, server logs, or email threads are inadmissible in court unless accompanied by a statutory certificate signed by the device custodian.

Criminal Procedure FACT 06

Section 528 BNSS (482 CrPC) Threshold

Under Bhajan Lal guidelines, the High Court exercises inherent powers to quash an FIR where the allegations, taken at face value, do not constitute any criminal offence, or where criminal machinery is weaponized to settle civil accounts.
